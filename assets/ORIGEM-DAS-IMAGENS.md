Geradas pela ferramenta integrada **Imagegen** e convertidas em WebP para o projeto. Não há dependência de serviços de imagem para executar o site.

## Prompt final — campanha

Landscape wide 3:2 photograph, sophisticated young adult Afro-Brazilian woman with natural curly dark hair and warm brown skin wearing bold translucent honey/olive acetate eyeglasses with clear optical lenses, cream linen shirt. Medium close up head and shoulders positioned toward the right, warm natural late afternoon sunlight, muted olive green studio backdrop. Elegant authentic relaxed confident expression, looking toward camera. Premium magazine photography, realistic skin texture, soft shadow. No text, no logos, no watermark. Image will occupy the right half of a deep blue and lime-green website hero.
